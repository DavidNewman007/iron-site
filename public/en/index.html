What we fix

Apple only. That is the whole point — we are not a general phone kiosk.

◉

Cracked screens

iPhone, iPad and MacBook displays. The most common holiday accident, and usually a same-day fix if the part is in stock.

◎

Water damage

Sea water, pool, rain, spilled coffee. Bring the device as soon as possible and do not try to charge it — that is what usually kills the board.

◈

Batteries

Battery draining by lunchtime, phone shutting down in the cold or in the heat. Replacement takes under an hour in most cases.

▦

MacBook repair

Keyboards, hinges, dust cleaning and thermal service, charging ports, board-level repair after liquid damage.

◌

Free diagnostics

In most cases we tell you what is wrong and what it costs before you commit to anything.

Diagnostics may be charged for iMac and Apple Watch disassembly, and for liquid damage, where the outcome cannot be judged without partial repair.

⌾

Buying and selling

New and second-hand iPhone and MacBook, trade-in. Handy if a device is beyond economical repair while you are travelling.
