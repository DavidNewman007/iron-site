The three things that happen on holiday

These are what people bring us most often in season. If yours is one of them, you already know what to expect.

◉

Cracked screen

Usually about an hour, if the display for your model is in stock. We keep the popular iPhone models on the shelf; for rarer ones the part is ordered and typically arrives within a few days.

Bring the device with you — we quote precisely only after seeing whether the touch layer and the frame survived.

◎

Water damage

Come immediately, and do not switch it on. Sea water is the worst case: salt keeps corroding the board for as long as it stays inside. Cleaning takes from a few hours to a couple of days depending on the damage.

This is the one case where diagnostics may be charged — the outcome cannot be judged without opening and cleaning the board first.

◈

Battery dying by lunchtime

Under an hour for most iPhone models. Also worth checking if the phone shuts down suddenly at 30%, or dies quickly in the heat.

    Before you come

    Back up if you still can

    If the device turns on, back it up to iCloud or a laptop. We are careful with data, but a repair is never a guarantee that a failing device keeps it.

    Know your Apple ID

    After many repairs the device must be unlocked and signed in. Without the Apple ID password we cannot verify that the repair worked — and neither can you.

    Water: do not charge it

    The most common way a rescuable phone becomes unrescuable is being plugged in while still wet. No rice, no hairdryer, no charging — just bring it.

    Message ahead if the model is rare

    For older or unusual models we may need to order the part. A message the day before can save you a second trip across town.

    Common questions

    Do you speak English?

    Our technicians speak Russian. Enquiries in English we handle by text — WhatsApp, Telegram, email — and reply in English. In the workshop we use written translation, which works perfectly well for describing a fault and agreeing a price.

    Are you an official Apple service?

    No. We are an independent workshop and we are not affiliated with Apple Inc. That is why we can repair devices that are out of warranty, discontinued, or would be refused by an authorised centre.

    How do I pay?

    In Russian roubles, by cash or by card. Note that foreign-issued Visa and Mastercard do not work in Russia — please have cash if your cards are not Russian.

    Can you keep it while I am at the beach?

    Yes. Leave the device with us, we message you the moment it is ready. Most repairs finish the same day.
