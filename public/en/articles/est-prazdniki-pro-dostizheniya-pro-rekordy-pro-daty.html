There are holidays for achievements, for records, for dates

17 October 2026

And today is about those who bring things back to life. International Repair Day.

To repair is not just to replace a part. It is to see years of use still ahead behind a cracked screen. To understand that equipment written off the books can keep serving. And to choose repair when it is easier to say "buy a new one".

To be a technician means holding in your hands not hardware, but someone's photos, messages and plans. So today our words go to everyone who repairs and who does not abandon broken things. May things break less often, be repaired more easily, and may every working day end with working equipment and steady hands.

We invite everyone involved for coffee — Moskovskaya, 5. IRON SERVICE
