The iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone Ultra are being announced next month, and insiders are already stirring up interest

17 August 2026

A fresh leak describes what to expect from the new A20 Pro chip. According to supply chain sources, the performance gain will be noticeable: the CPU side becomes roughly 15–20% faster, and the graphics pull ahead by even more.

Interestingly, particular emphasis is placed on efficiency. The A20 Pro is said to deliver more power at the same or even lower consumption. For anyone who cares about battery life, that is excellent news. An improved neural engine is expected as well: noticeably more powerful, which should speed up Apple Intelligence and heavy applications.

Numbers on paper and real-world feel are, of course, different things, but the intrigue is building. These devices are clearly going to be quick. We will wait for the official presentation and then see how it all feels in practice. And if one of your older devices decides to give up at the worst possible moment — we are always nearby.
