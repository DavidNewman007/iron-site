A teacher is someone who explained to you not just logarithms

5 October 2026

He showed that a mistake in a dictation is not a catastrophe, but a chance to think again. That you can not know something, but it is shameful not to ask. And that a strict look is often the same care, just wrapped up more tightly.

Thank you for your patience, which is worth more than any programmes. For the ability to hear a quiet question from the back row. For the fact that you do not just pass on knowledge, but help people not to be afraid to use it.

Today is your day. Let it be at least a little quieter than usual, with warm words and the rest you truly deserve.

But our work at the service centre is about the same thing, only with hardware: to explain how something works, and to make it work again. Every time someone brings us a beloved gadget in tears and panic, we become a bit like teachers: we calm them down, we work it out, we show them that nothing terrible has happened. Because a simple teaching rule applies to technology too — a careful explanation heals better than punishment.

May there be more bright lessons than harsh ones in your life.

With respect, the iron guys at IRON SERVICE
