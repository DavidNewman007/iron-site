That very case when the brand-new box seems to reach for your hand by itself, but a voice inside whispers:…

5 September 2026

That very case when the brand-new box seems to reach for your hand by itself, but a voice inside whispers: “First let’s count the tasks, then spend the money” 🧾

Mac mini M6 is a machine for those who load the processor with typical work scenarios: editing in a couple of streams, colour grading without a hundred layers, neural networks that can live in ordinary memory. This little one handles a 4K timeline without panicking and doesn’t ask for a separate socket with attitude.

The M5 Pro, though, is needed when the project resembles a high-rise: timelines tens of minutes long, noise reduction on the fly, local AI models that feel cramped in the standard amount of memory. What matters here is not just speed but also headroom — so that rendering doesn’t turn coffee into your only entertainment.

What you should look at is not the letter in the name but your own habits: if in the evening the project goes off to export within an hour — the M6 is enough. If there’s no time to wait and the machine has to grind away without days off — take the Pro, but already with a memory and cooling upgrade, not the bare version off the shelf.

And before you send the old warrior to the scrap heap, drop in for a diagnostic: sometimes the question of “getting a new one” is settled by replacing the thermal paste and cleaning out the dust that has been choking the cooler for the last six months 🔧
