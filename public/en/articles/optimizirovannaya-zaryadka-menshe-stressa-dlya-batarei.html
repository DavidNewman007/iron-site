Optimized charging: less stress on the battery

24 November 2026

The "Optimized Charging" feature helps extend battery life by reducing the time the device spends at a full charge. The iPhone pauses charging at 80% and completes the process just before you typically unplug the cable. The system activates when it predicts a long connection, such as overnight. The algorithm needs time to learn your habits for stable operation, so the feature works best in places where you spend a lot of time, such as home or the office.

To manage the setting, open "Settings" and go to the "Battery" section. On iPhone 15 and newer, select "Charging," where you can enable or disable the option. On iPhone 14 and earlier models, the path is through "Battery Health & Charging." If you urgently need a full charge, the parameter can be temporarily disabled.

This is a simple way to care for battery longevity without extra effort.
