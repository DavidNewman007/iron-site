iPhone 18 Pro Max in a test on the Xiaobai’s Tech Reviews channel charged from zero to 100% in 55 minutes

18 September 2026

The previous model, iPhone 17 Pro Max, took 81 minutes for the same — almost one and a half times the difference.

It's not just about the battery. In the first 12 minutes, the new device hit a peak of 52 W, whereas last year's model capped at 36 W. So it's the moment when the phone is most "hungry" that has become faster — and that's exactly the minutes when we usually get ready and rush out.

For the owner, this means a simple thing: the ritual of "put it on charge and wait" is shortened, especially if you charge in snatches before going out. But fast charging has a price — more heat on the brick, cable and the board itself. If the device gets hotter than usual or the percentage creeps up slower than in the first weeks, that's a reason to show it to a technician. Our workshops are exactly for such cases. 🔌
