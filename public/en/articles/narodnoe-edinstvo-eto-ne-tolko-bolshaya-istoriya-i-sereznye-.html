People's unity is not only grand history and serious words

4 November 2026

It's when a neighbour tells you where best to get a phone repaired. When a stranger holds the door for you on the metro. When an old friend writes: "So how are you doing there?" — and your heart warms at once.

On days like these it's especially clear: what unites us is what is genuine. Millions of simple, dependable acts by people across the whole country — from Kaliningrad to Kamchatka. That is our character. Calm, good-natured, able to lend a shoulder without loud words.

May everything in your home work as it should, and may life give you more reasons to gather together. A warm holiday to you and your loved ones.

With respect, the iron guys at IRON SERVICE
