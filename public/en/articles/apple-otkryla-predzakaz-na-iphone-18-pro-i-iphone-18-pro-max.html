📱 Apple has opened pre-orders for the iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max — you can order directly from…

13 September 2026

📱 Apple has opened pre-orders for the iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max — you can order directly from Apple, as well as from Walmart. The devices themselves were shown on Wednesday.

The main points from the announcement: the camera has gained a variable aperture system — this means it has become easier to shoot in different lighting and to control depth of field. The Dynamic Island has become smaller, meaning the cutout in the screen takes up less space and interferes less with the picture. Plus four new colours to choose from — everyone can pick one to suit them.

A funny detail: the pre-order started before the first reviews and hands-on experience had time to spread online. So you'll have to choose based on pictures and Apple's promises.
