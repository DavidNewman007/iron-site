On 4 October 1957, a man-made signal left Earth for space for the first time

4 October 2026

A small satellite in orbit — and essentially, the beginning of a new era. Decades later, this day became a holiday for those who look after that very sky from which that very signal first came.

Happy Space Forces Day.

You are the ones who work above the everyday bustle: in orbits, behind radar screens, at communications consoles. Where the cost of a mistake is measured not in hours or in roubles, but in the calm sky over everyone's heads across the country.

Thank you for seeing beyond the horizon. Steady shifts, accurate data and silence on the air — the kind that says better than any words that everything is working as it should.

Happy holiday. IRON SERVICE
