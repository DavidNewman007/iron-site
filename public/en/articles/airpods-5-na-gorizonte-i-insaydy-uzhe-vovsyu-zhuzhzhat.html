AirPods 5 on the horizon — and the leaks are already buzzing

25 August 2026

Bloomberg reports: at the expected September Apple event, two models of the 'fifth' generation of earbuds may be presented. This is not about cameras — those, according to rumours, are being kept for next year. But an update to sound, chips, and possibly the shape — is a very real scenario.

Why does this matter? If the leaks are correct, the choice will expand again: some will take the base version, some — the advanced one with noise cancellation. The main question is how quickly the battery will say 'thanks' for active use. Batteries in earbuds are a consumable item, and it is normal for them to wear out faster than the earbuds themselves.

So if your current 'buds' are living out their last weeks, it might be worth holding off until the announcement. And if they have already passed away — well, we are always nearby to bring the old fighter back to life. 😉
