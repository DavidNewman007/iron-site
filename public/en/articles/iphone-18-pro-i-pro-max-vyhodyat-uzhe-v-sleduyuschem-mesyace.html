iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max are coming out next month, and besides the internals, we can expect three notable...

20 August 2026

iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max are coming out next month, and besides the internals, we can expect three notable design changes. According to rumours, the engineers have finally decided to give the look a bit of a refresh, and here is what insiders are saying. 🕵️♂️

First is the new frame: the body will be thinner and have more rounded corners, so it will be noticeably more comfortable to hold. Second are the buttons: it looks like the mechanical keys will be replaced with touch-sensitive ones with additional haptic feedback, like on the latest models. Third is the camera placement: the module may move to make room for an improved lens array.

We are waiting for the official presentation, but if you happen to want to 'refresh' your current device to an 'as new' state, we are always ready to work on its colour palette. 🔧✨
