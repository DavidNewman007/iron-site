Friends, today is 30 September — Internet Day in Russia

30 September 2026

The holiday is young, but it feels as if the network has always been with us. We can no longer remember how we lived without video calls with relatives in other cities, without the morning news over coffee, and without answers to any question within a minute.

The internet has erased distances and made the world shared. Ideas are born in it, music plays, someone finds work, and someone finds kindred spirits. And each of us has our own corner in this digital universe, our own feed, our own rhythm.

May your network always have a stable signal, interesting conversation partners, and no packet loss. Happy Internet Day!
