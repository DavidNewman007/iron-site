The back glass on an iPhone 16 Pro has cracked

20 October 2026

Annoying, but not fatal — it is not expensive to replace, and the phone keeps working. It seems like the question of one trip to a repair shop. But this is exactly where people most often burn another 20 thousand.

The glass on the 16 Pro is glued to the body properly. To remove it, the technician heats the smartphone, prises it up with a spudger and slowly runs it around the perimeter. There is an invisible catch in this process: the frame is thin, the adhesive is stiff, and under the glass there is no safety cushion but the board, cameras and flex cables. One sharp movement warps the body. At that moment, on the opposite side of the phone, at the edge of the display, the OLED takes a mechanical hit that neither a crack nor a scratch will report. The screen simply goes dark. Forever.

Then the real maths begins: a repair that was "cheap and quick" turns into a display replacement. And if the back glass cost pennies, the OLED on the iPhone 16 Pro will drag the budget into a completely different league. In the end the owner leaves a sum at the repair shop that they never expected even to see.

How to avoid this is a question of method. The back glass on the 16 Pro can be removed carefully: nothing snapped off in jerks, no overheating, and each zone approached at its own angle. Before this procedure we always assess the condition of the body and the bonding of the glass. The fragile points of the display are protected — for example, the screen is lifted so that it does not breathe hot air at close range while the glass becomes soft. It takes longer, but it is predictable.

Usually the question is: "how much does it cost to remove and replace the glass?" But ending up in a situation where a display is also needed, as after a careless intervention, is no longer a question of misfortune but a question of choice. Twenty thousand for a lesson that could have been avoided.

In autumn such stories become more frequent in Sochi. The beach season is over, people have dug into pockets, boots and bedside tables — and found cracks, spiderwebs and chips on their summer iPhones. If you see similar beauty on the back of your 16 Pro — do not take it to those who promise "in twenty minutes, almost free". Replacing the back glass on an iPhone 16 Pro has long been well practised and takes a predictable amount of time. Just glass, just hands, just an intact display.
