What the “Battery Health” item hides

23 November 2026

<b>What the “Battery Health” item hides</b>

iPhone owners should know that information about battery capacity is available in the device settings. For iPhone 15 and newer models, Apple provides extended data: in addition to the overall system status, it displays the number of charge cycles, the manufacturing date, and the date battery use began. On iPhone 14 and earlier devices, you must select the “Battery Health and Charging” item to access this information.

On iPhone Duo, detailed information is available for each battery separately, and the data may differ under normal use. It is important to understand that battery life and the number of charge cycles depend directly on the device’s usage mode and settings. Service or recycling of an iPhone battery is performed only by Apple or an Apple Authorized Service Provider.

This data helps track the battery’s lifecycle without guesswork. If you want to check the current status of your device, look in the corresponding settings section. This is a simple way to get official information from the manufacturer.
