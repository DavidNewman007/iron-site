Charging limit: protecting the battery

25 November 2026

The "Charging Limit" feature is available on iPhone 15 and newer. It allows you to set a maximum charge level, such as 80%, to reduce battery wear. This is especially relevant if you frequently plug your phone into the mains or leave it charging for a long time.

To set the limit, open "Settings", go to the "Battery" section, and select "Charging". There, you need to specify the desired percentage. When the limit is active, the device stops charging at the set mark but periodically reaches 100%. The limit can be changed or disabled at any time. If you tap "Allow until tomorrow", the restriction will take effect again at 6:00.

The setup is simple and flexible, helping to extend the battery's life without complex procedures. Just choose a suitable scenario and monitor the battery's condition.
